Senior Kindergarten (Sr. KG) Course Information

Our Senior Kindergarten programme is designed to build upon the foundational skills developed in Junior Kindergarten. Here’s an overview:

Curriculum

  1. Language and Literacy: Developing reading, writing, and communication skills.
  2. Mathematics: Building numeracy skills, problem-solving, and critical thinking.
  3. Science and Technology: Exploring the natural world, scientific concepts, and basic technology skills.
  4. Social Studies: Learning about community, culture, and basic geography.
  5. Art and Music: Encouraging creative expression and appreciation.

Assessment and Evaluation

  1. Regular Assessments: Ongoing evaluations to monitor progress.
  2. Parent-Teacher Conferences: Regular meetings to discuss your child’s development.

Goals

  1. Prepare for Grade 1: Build a strong foundation for future academic success.
  2. Develop independence: Encourage self-reliance, responsibility, and confidence.
  3. Foster a love for learning: Encourage curiosity, creativity, and exploration.

Our Senior Kindergarten programme is designed to provide a supportive and engaging environment that prepares children for future academic success.
