Playgroup Course Information

Playgroup is a fun and interactive way for young children to learn and develop essential skills. Here’s an overview of what your child can expect:

Course Objectives

  1. Socialization: Encourage social interaction, sharing, and cooperation.
  2. Cognitive Development: Foster curiosity, problem-solving, and creativity.
  3. Emotional Intelligence: Develop self-awareness, self-regulation, and empathy.
  4. Fine and Gross Motor Skills: Enhance physical development through play.

Activities

  1. Play-Based Learning: Hands-on activities, games, and play to facilitate learning.
  2. Arts and Crafts: Creative expression through various art forms.
  3. Music and Movement: Fun activities to promote physical development and creativity.
  4. Story Time: Interactive storytelling to foster language and literacy skills.

Benefits

  1. Preparation for Preschool: Playgroup helps children transition smoothly to preschool.
  2. Develops Social Skills: Encourages interaction with peers and adults.
  3. Builds Confidence: Fosters self-esteem and confidence through play and exploration.
  4. Supports Cognitive Development: Enhances problem-solving and critical thinking skills.

What to Expect

  1. Fun and Engaging Activities: Playgroup activities are designed to be enjoyable and interactive.
  2. Supportive Environment: A nurturing and supportive environment that encourages exploration and learning.
  3. Qualified Staff: Experienced staff who are passionate about early childhood education.

By enrolling your child in Playgroup, you’ll be providing them with a solid foundation for future academic success and social development.
