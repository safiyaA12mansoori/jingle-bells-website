About us

A neighbourhood school with a big heart

Jingle Bell's is a co-educational preschool in Kondhwa Budruk, built around play, safety and the first friendships that shape school life.

Our story

Families in Laxmi Nagar and Kapil Nagar trust us for an English-medium, play-way beginning. We keep groups small enough to notice every child, and days simple enough that little ones feel secure.

From the first ring of the morning bell to home time, our team focuses on language, motor skills, social grace and the joy of coming to school.

From the Director

Welcome to Jingle Bell's

Dear students, parents and guardians — I am thrilled to welcome you to our school community. As Director, I am committed to a supportive, inclusive environment that fosters academic excellence, creativity and personal growth.

Vision statement

My vision is to create a nurturing and inclusive learning environment that empowers young minds to grow, explore and thrive. I aim to:

  1. Foster a love for learning that lasts a lifetime
  2. Encourage creativity, curiosity and critical thinking
  3. Develop confident, compassionate and responsible individuals
  4. Build strong relationships between students, teachers and the community
  5. Provide a safe and supportive space for children to grow and flourish

Be your best self

  1. Always be kind to others.
  2. Listen to your teachers and parents.
  3. Try your best in everything you do.
  4. Make new friends and be inclusive.
  5. Believe in yourself and your abilities.

Learn and grow

  1. Ask questions and seek answers.
  2. Explore your interests and passions.
  3. Practice and persevere when things get tough.
  4. Learn from your mistakes.
  5. Celebrate your successes.

Stay safe and have fun

  1. Wash your hands often; eat well and rest.
  2. Be kind to your body and mind.
  3. Ask for help when you need it.
  4. Play, imagine, read and explore.
  5. Smile and laugh often.

A message to parents

As we begin this academic year, I welcome you to our school community. Our teachers and staff are committed to a supportive, inclusive environment that fosters academic excellence, creativity and personal growth.

Our priorities

  1. Student safety and well-being: We prioritise the physical, emotional and mental well-being of all students.
  2. Academic excellence: We strive for excellence through a thoughtful curriculum and innovative teaching.
  3. Community engagement: We encourage parental involvement, community partnerships and student leadership.

We value open communication between home and school. Please reach out to your child’s teacher or our office with questions or concerns. Together we can help our young learners reach their full potential.

Best regards,
Director, Jingle Bell's Pre-Primary & Nursery School

The journey

Growing with Kondhwa's children

A quiet timeline of how a local preschool becomes part of family stories — one academic year at a time.

  1. A school of firsts

    Playgroup and Nursery open as a warm welcome for toddlers taking their earliest steps away from home.

  2. Building Junior & Senior KG

    Literacy, numeracy and school routines are added without losing songs, art and outdoor play.

  3. A trusted neighbourhood name

    Parents recommend us for care, communication and a 12:1 ratio that keeps teaching personal.

  4. Today

    We continue to welcome new families on a first-come, first-served basis — ready for the next little learner.
