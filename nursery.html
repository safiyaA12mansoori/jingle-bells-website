Nursery School Information

Our nursery programme is designed for young children and focuses on providing a nurturing and engaging environment that supports their overall development. Here’s an overview:

Curriculum

  1. Play-Based Learning: Hands-on activities and play to facilitate learning.
  2. Social-Emotional Development: Encouraging social interaction, sharing, and cooperation.
  3. Cognitive Development: Fostering curiosity, problem-solving, and creativity.
  4. Fine and Gross Motor Skills: Enhancing physical development through play.

Activities

  1. Arts and Crafts: Creative expression through various art forms.
  2. Music and Movement: Fun activities to promote physical development and creativity.
  3. Story Time: Interactive storytelling to foster language and literacy skills.
  4. Sensory Play: Exploring textures, colours, and materials.

Benefits

  1. Early Childhood Development: Supports physical, emotional, and cognitive growth.
  2. Socialisation: Encourages interaction with peers and adults.
  3. Preparation for Preschool: Helps children transition smoothly to preschool.

Staff

  1. Qualified Teachers: Experienced staff with expertise in early childhood education.
  2. Supportive Environment: A nurturing and supportive environment that encourages exploration and learning.

Our nursery programme is designed to provide a solid foundation for young children, setting them up for future success and a lifelong love of learning.
