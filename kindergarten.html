Kindergarten School Information

Kindergarten is a foundational year that lays the groundwork for future academic success. Here’s an overview of what your child can expect to learn:

Curriculum Areas

  1. Language and Literacy: Phonics, reading, writing, vocabulary, and comprehension.
  2. Mathematics: Numbers, shapes, patterns, measurement, and basic arithmetic operations.
  3. Science: Exploration of the natural world, seasons, animals, and plants.
  4. Social Studies: Family, community, culture, and basic geography.
  5. Art and Music: Creative expression through various art forms, music, and movement.

Assessment and Evaluation

  1. Ongoing Assessments: Regular evaluations to monitor progress.
  2. Parent-Teacher Conferences: Regular meetings to discuss your child’s development.

Goals

  1. Build a strong foundation: Prepare your child for future academic success.
  2. Foster a love for learning: Encourage curiosity, creativity, and exploration.
  3. Develop social and emotional skills: Help your child become a confident, empathetic, and responsible individual.

By providing a nurturing and engaging environment, our kindergarten programme aims to set your child up for a lifelong love of learning.
